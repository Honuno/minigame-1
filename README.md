# Minigame 1
## Devlog
  Imagine a Scene as an empty painting canvas. The canvas provides the frame and the workspace where your picture will live, essentially your game, and will hold all the different elements together in one place. A GameObject is like an invisible sticky note that's placed on this canvas. On its own, it's just a clear sticky note, it does nothing and shows nothing. It is just there to mark a specific spot on that canvas. Components are the actual paints, stickers, and tools you will use to stick onto that clear sticky note. Adding a paint Component gives you color and shape, while a motion Component makes it slide across the canvas. Without Components, the note will just remain invisible, but with Components, it turns into a real, active part of the painting.
